Paste a narrative. It is cleaned the same way as the training data.

The cause is stamped, a photo shows what it looks like, and SHAP shows which words decided it.

  • The app's own retrained layer scores 57.9% accuracy and 0.522 macro-F1, close to the Colab run.
  • About 6% of reports are longer than BERT's 512-token limit and get cut off.
  • Rare causes are the least reliable. Treat a prediction as a second opinion.
  • It runs on an Oracle Cloud VM with one setup script.
